Warning Signs for Storm Damage Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • After a weather event, insulation inside walls or overhead feels soaked
  • Damaged or displaced flashing around roof penetrations, valleys or edges
  • Water getting past doors, windows and wall penetrations when wind pushes the rain
  • Puddles near skylights, beside a chimney or on the attic floor
  • Rooms that start to smell stale or moldy following a storm
  • Downspouts knocked off and gutter runs hanging after high wind
  • Bubbling paint and soft, drooping spots overhead once the rain stops

How to Prevent Storm Damage Repair Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Cracked caulk lets blown rain in, so inspect and renew the seals on doors and windows
  • Photograph your property once a year so its condition is on record for insurance purposes
  • Extend downspouts and slope the soil so runoff moves away from the foundation
  • Before storm season each year, get the roof inspected and its problem areas repaired
  • Clogged gutters overflow in a downpour, so clear them and the downspouts often
  • Branches break in high wind, so keep nearby trees pruned away from the roof and siding
